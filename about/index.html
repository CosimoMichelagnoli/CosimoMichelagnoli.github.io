About

I'm an Offensive Cybersecurity Analyst based in Milan, Italy, currently working at Iliad Italia on infrastructure, web/API and Active Directory penetration testing, plus red and purple teaming activities in coordination with the SOC. Before that, I worked as a Security Analyst at HN Security in Turin.

I hold an MSc in Cybersecurity from Politecnico di Torino, where I focused on cryptography, security-by-design and cloud/Kubernetes infrastructure — a passion that still shows in how I approach engagements today.

Outside of client work, I like breaking things I've built myself: from a homemade OS161 kernel, to a Kubernetes sandbox platform (CrownLabs), to a quantum-resistant blockchain thesis project. You can read the long version on Story, or the structured one on my CV.

When I'm not testing infrastructure, I'm usually either on a rugby pitch or flying a paraglider — both activities where skipping the preparation tends to end badly, which maps pretty well to what I do for a living.
